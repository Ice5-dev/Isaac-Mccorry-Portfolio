01 / AI systems

Prooflane

Active build

How much autonomy should a business give an AI agent—and what evidence would justify that decision?

The idea

Prooflane is being developed as a simulated environment for observing an agent's actions before it receives broader company permissions.

The assessment

The intended output identifies the autonomy level the agent demonstrates, where it falls short, and what should improve while it remains in shadow mode.

Current stage

Backend evaluation work and repeated iterations are underway. A public product demonstration and validated customer results are not presented here yet.

What I'm learning

Trust needs specific evidence, clear failure boundaries, and a record of how a system behaved—not just a compelling demo.
